The railway station of Kom-loh is located in Baranya County, in the heart of the Mehchek Mountains. It stands near the city center with a direct connection to the bus station. Its history dates back to eighteen ninety-seven. Kom-loh is the terminus of line forty-seven from Dom-boh-vahr, reaching the city via Goh-dee-shah. Built primarily for coal mining, the railway played a major role in the town's industrial past. Today, the Kom-loh-ver-zum cultural center showcases this rich heritage. Thanks to the nearby Mehchek hills, the station is also a great starting point for hikers and visitors exploring the region.
