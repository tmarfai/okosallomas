Why visit Komló?

Komló is a mining town in Baranya, shaped by the Mecsek hills, its industrial heritage and newer cultural attractions.

Line 47 · Komló station

Station History In Brief

Komló is the terminus of the Dombóvár-Komló railway line. The line has seen more than one closure and reopening, so its story is not a single stop-and-start event.

47

Dombóvár-Komló line

Komló is the end station of railway line 47. The line reaches the town from Godisa, and the station's chainage marker is 187.

HÉV II

Visible on old postcards

Old photos and postcards show that Komló once had a second-class local railway station building.

1975

MÁV social building

According to the station photo archive, the MÁV social building in Komló was opened at the end of 1975.

2009

Passenger service stops

From 13 December 2009, at the timetable change, passenger service on the line towards Komló was discontinued.

2010-2011

Trains return

Passenger trains returned from 12 December 2010, and the ticket office reopened on 4 July 2011.

2023-2026

Another pause and return

Passenger service stopped again on 1 August 2023. From 1 August 2026, trains return on a two-hour pattern.

That is why Komló's railway story is not a simple closure and reopening, but a recurring chapter in the life of line 47.
